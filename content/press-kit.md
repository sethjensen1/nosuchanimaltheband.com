## Electronic Press Kit

No Such Animal is a home recorded rock band that started in Utah. The band consists of Finn Morrison (frontman, vocals), Cole Harris (guitar), Ella Morrison (keys), and Seth Jensen (bass). Their high-energy riffs and catchy melodies create a distinct blend of punk rock, emo, and progressive rock.

In 2026, they released “I’m Not Angry, You’re Angry,” a punk-rock album with medieval flair which distills the frustration of being let down by society into 12 hard-hitting, quick, emotional tracks.

{{< musicshowcase data_key="press-kit-showcase" >}}

No Such Animal regularly performs across Utah and in nearby states, playing in Salt Lake City, Phoenix, Logan, St. George, and at festivals such as Fork Fest and Buzzards & Bees. Following the success of their single, “Witches in Salt Lake City,” they released “The Entertainer,” an album about making sense of things in a world of witches, spacemen, and cyborgs. Their music has amassed over 2,500,000 streams.
